'''{{cite web|url=http://krns.org|title=The Bible-smith Project}}''' is the project that works on any kind of [[Bible]] related applications and information for more effective and efficient [[Evangelism]] with the vision '''"The Gospel for Everyone"''', making it universally accessible and useful.

From the project, more than 20,000 [[Gospel]] related contents and more than 200 android/iOS applications - including Bibles in more than 70 different languages - have been developed and/or shared to the world since 1997.
<br>
== About Bible ==
=== The Bible ===
- is the work of Holy Spirit through His people.<br>
- acknowledges that you have Holy Spirit within you.<br>
- acknowledges Holy Spirit being the original author and He is still living.<Br>
The Holy Spirit is the original author of all Bibles.<br>

=== About Gospel ===
Testifications/witnesses provided in scripture about Jesus Christ are facts/truth.<br>
All texts/contents in the Scripture testify about Jesus Christ either directly or indirectly [John 5:39].<br>
Only through faith in Jesus Christ and by grace a person have salvation; and that not of yourselves: it is given by God not by works [Ephesians 2:8].<br>
